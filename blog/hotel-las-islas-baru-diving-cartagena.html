Hotel Las Islas in Barú and diving the Rosario Islands: a guide from Cartagena

Reefs about 45 minutes away by boat, a hotel among the mangroves and, on the way back, a night in the walled city.

Leer en español →

Cartagena has two faces: the walled city and the sea around it. The best way to know both is to split the trip: a few days on the islands and a few nights in the Old City.

Hotel Las Islas

In Barú, at the end of the Ciénaga de Cholón and about 45 minutes by speedboat from Cartagena, Hotel Las Islas is one of the best-known luxury hotels in the area. It sits among mangroves next to the Corales del Rosario y San Bernardo National Natural Park, so the sea is the main event: kayaking, canoeing, mangrove tours and trips to nearby islets.

Diving the Rosario Islands

The national park protects coral reefs that are among the most visited in the Colombian Caribbean. Underwater you'll find hard coral, sponges and colourful fish such as surgeonfish, parrotfish and angelfish, plus turtles, octopus and lobster. Well-known dive sites include the Isla Pavitos wall, Isla Gloria and the San Martín de Pajarales wreck.

  • Beginners: discovery dives and snorkelling in shallow water, no certification needed.
  • Certified divers: dives range from a few metres to several tens of metres depending on the site; visibility changes with the weather and season.
  • Who to go with: always book with a certified dive centre, which will match dives to your level. If you're staying at Las Islas, ask the hotel about trips.
  • Look after the reef: don't touch the coral, use biodegradable sunscreen and follow park rules.
Back to the city

After the sea, the night belongs to the Old City. Casa Salvaje is at Calle Santa Teresa #32-41: fire at Kanibal, dinner and, Thursday to Saturday, the club at SLVJ. See opening hours.

How to plan the trip

An order that works: land in Cartagena and spend a night or two in the walled city; head to Barú for sea and diving days; then return to the Old City for your last night. That way the trip ends with a long dinner and, at the weekend, the club until 3 am. Always leave a day without diving before you fly.
