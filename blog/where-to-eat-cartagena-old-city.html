Where to eat in Cartagena's Old City: one house, four worlds

A long lunch, a late dinner or a night that refuses to end: it all fits inside one colonial house two blocks from Plaza Santo Domingo.

Leer en español →

Eating in Cartagena's Old City comes with an advantage few places can match: everything is a short walk away, between balconies, bougainvillea and stone streets. The hard part is choosing. At Casa Salvaje we solved it differently: instead of one restaurant, one colonial house with four worlds, each with its own kitchen, its own music and its own way of spending the evening.

Kanibal: fire and instinct

Kanibal is the world of fire. Meat over flame, vegetables pulled from the coals and a drink poured without asking. It's the place for an unhurried lunch or a long dinner with friends. Every dish carries its mark: the red glyphs are the house alphabet.

Open Monday to Saturday from 12 noon to 12 midnight, and Sundays from 11 am to 7 pm. Meet Kanibal.

SLVJ: a feast for earthly gods

Stone, velvet, amber light and a sushi bar to open the night. SLVJ is the house's most dressed-up dining room: come for a slow dinner and, Thursday to Saturday, stay as the room turns into a club until 3 am.

Open Monday to Saturday 12–3 pm and 7 pm–12 am, and Sundays 11 am–7 pm. Meet SLVJ.

La Domenica and ONO: what's next

La Domenica will be the daytime world: coffee when you wake, pizza for a very long lunch and aperitivo as the afternoon fades. ONO, the hosomaki bar born in Bogotá, arrives in the house between November and December 2026. To hear first, join the guest list.

The essentials

Calle Santa Teresa #32-41, where Baloco meets Santa Teresa, two blocks from Plaza Santo Domingo. Every opening time, with each world's live status, is in the hours section.

Tips for eating in the Old City

  • Book ahead, especially Thursday to Saturday. Weekend nights fill up; WhatsApp is the fastest way.
  • Lunch late. The midday heat calls for a table indoors and a long sobremesa; the city wakes up again when the sun drops.
  • Walk. The Old City is made for walking, and the airport is about 15 minutes from our door.
  • Leave the night open. At dinner, behave. After dinner, anything.
