More boutique hotels in Cartagena: Casa San Agustín, Casa Pestagua, Quadrifolio and Getsemaní

An aqueduct in the courtyard, a 17th-century mansion and Getsemaní houses covered in murals: another round of hotels with history.

Leer en español →

We've already covered Casa del Arzobispado, Santa Clara and Charleston Santa Teresa. Here's another round of boutique hotels, inside and outside the walls, for anyone who wants to sleep in a house with a past.

Casa San Agustín

Three restored colonial houses in the walled city, joined around a stretch of the city's old aqueduct that now runs beside the pool. A member of The Leading Hotels of the World and one of the most elegant addresses in the Old City.

Casa Pestagua

An aristocratic colonial mansion turned hotel, with courtyards and generous suites. In 2023 it became the first Relais & Châteaux member in Colombia and was named the best historic hotel on the continent.

Hotel Quadrifolio

In San Diego, the quietest corner of the Old City, a colonial house with a handful of individually decorated rooms, a leafy courtyard, a pool and a rooftop hot tub. Its name comes from the four-leaf motif found in colonial architecture.

Getsemaní: Casa del Coliseo and Casa Lola

Just outside the walls, Getsemaní is the neighbourhood of murals, street music and Plaza de la Trinidad. Casa del Coliseo is a luxury boutique with murals and a pool; Casa Lola joins a 17th-century colonial house with a late-19th-century building, with a rooftop terrace and pools.

From any of them

All are a walk or a short ride from Casa Salvaje, at Calle Santa Teresa #32-41. Check opening hours and book your table.
