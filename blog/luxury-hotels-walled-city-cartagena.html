Luxury hotels in Cartagena's walled city: Townhouse, Casa del Arzobispado, Bastión, Casa Pestagua and more

Rooftop pools, colonial courtyards and houses with a handful of rooms: six hotels inside the walls, all a walk from Casa Salvaje.

Leer en español →

Sleeping inside the walled city changes the trip: you walk out at night, stroll home after dinner and wake to the Cathedral bells. These are six hotels we recommend, from the smallest to the most complete.

Townhouse

Steps from Plaza Fernández de Madrid, a handful of rooms and two bars: the rooftop, with plunge pools and views over the tiled roofs, and the lobby bar, which turns speakeasy with jazz at weekends. For anyone who wants atmosphere without leaving the hotel.

Casa del Arzobispado

Next to the Cathedral, in the former archbishop's residence, a hotel with few rooms and a very quiet atmosphere. On the same street is Época, one of our favourite cafés to start the day.

Bastión Luxury Hotel

One of the largest luxury boutique hotels in the walled city, built on a colonial house that keeps its inner courtyards. It has a rooftop pool overlooking the Old City's domes, a restaurant, a spa and a gym, ideal if you want big-hotel service on a quiet street.

Casa Pestagua

An aristocratic colonial mansion turned hotel, with courtyards and generous suites. It was Colombia's first Relais & Châteaux member and was named the best historic hotel on the continent.

La Passion

An eight-room house with chequered stone floors, balconies and stairs climbing to a rooftop pool and bar with front-row views of the Cathedral.

Casa La Cartujita

On Calle del Curato, a restored colonial mansion with a few rooms and a small indoor pool. Intimate and quiet, for anyone who wants to feel the house is theirs.

From any of them to the table

All are a few blocks from Casa Salvaje, at Calle Santa Teresa #32-41. Kanibal opens at noon, SLVJ serves dinner from 7 pm and, Thursday to Saturday, the club runs until 3 am. See opening hours.
