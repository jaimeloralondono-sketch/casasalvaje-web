Must-try restaurants in Cartagena: from the Old City to Getsemaní

Chef-driven Caribbean cooking, legendary ceviche and walled-city classics: a list for eating well in Cartagena.

Leer en español →

Cartagena has become one of the most exciting places to eat in Colombia. This is our list for anyone coming here to eat well: a few classics, a few with international acclaim and, of course, our house.

Celele (Getsemaní)

Celele cooks the Colombian Caribbean with local ingredients, many of them wild-harvested, and a menu that changes constantly. It's one of the country's most celebrated restaurants on the Latin America's 50 Best list. Book ahead: it rarely takes walk-ins.

Carmen

Born in Medellín and in Cartagena since 2012, Carmen works with local ingredients, plenty of seafood and Asian touches in a tasting menu inside a colonial house in the Old City.

La Cevichería

On Calle Stuart in San Diego, it's the city's most famous ceviche spot ever since Anthony Bourdain came through. No reservations: arrive early or expect a short wait.

La Vitrola and Juan del Mar

Two institutions. La Vitrola, in a colonial house facing the city walls, with live Cuban music in the evenings. Juan del Mar, seafood on Plaza de San Diego, one of the liveliest squares in the Old City.

Kanibal and SLVJ, at Casa Salvaje

At Calle Santa Teresa #32-41, two blocks from Plaza Santo Domingo. Kanibal is fire: meat over flame, vegetables pulled from the coals. SLVJ is a feast and a sushi bar, and Thursday to Saturday the room becomes a club until 3 am.

Tip

In high season, book everything ahead. For our house, WhatsApp is the fastest way; check opening hours before heading out.
