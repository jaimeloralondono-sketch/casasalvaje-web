A Sunday in Cartagena: coffee, a long lunch and an afternoon in the Old City

Sunday in Cartagena calls for calm: good coffee, a long table and no rush.

Leer en español →

Sunday is the best day to discover the Old City at an easy pace. The streets wake up quieter and the plan is simple: eat well and walk.

Morning: coffee

Start with specialty coffee in the Old City; we keep a list in our café guide. For brunch, Bocagrande is a few minutes away.

Midday: a long lunch

On Sundays, Kanibal and SLVJ open from 11 am to 7 pm. It's the day for a lunch without a clock: fire at Kanibal or the SLVJ sushi bar, and a long sobremesa.

Afternoon: sunset

When the heat eases, walk to the city walls for sunset or take an electric carriage through the plazas.

Sunday before a holiday

When Monday is a public holiday, Kanibal and SLVJ stay open on Sunday until 12 am. See opening hours and the 2026–2027 long weekends.

Soon, La Domenica will be our Sunday world: coffee when you wake, pizza for a very long lunch and aperitivo as the afternoon fades.
