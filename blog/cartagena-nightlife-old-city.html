What to do at night in Cartagena's Old City: from sunset to the small hours

Night in Cartagena begins when the sun goes down. This is the route we'd take.

Leer en español →

By day, Cartagena is heat and light. By night, the walled city changes: lit balconies, full plazas and music drifting out of the houses. Here's how we'd build a full night.

5:30 pm · Sunset on the walls

Climb the city walls near Baluarte de Santo Domingo and watch the sun drop into the sea. It's the most Cartagena ritual there is.

6:30 pm · Through the plazas

Walk Plaza Santo Domingo, San Pedro Claver and Plaza de San Diego. If you'd rather sit, electric carriages leave from several points in the Old City until 11 pm.

7:30 pm · Dinner

At Casa Salvaje, two options: the fire of Kanibal or the feast at SLVJ, with a sushi bar to start. Book ahead, especially Thursday to Saturday.

11:30 pm · The club

Thursday to Saturday, once dinner winds down, the SLVJ room lights up on a Funktion-One system and the club runs until 3 am. You can reserve a club table in advance.

Good to know

At dinner, behave. After dinner, anything. We're at Calle Santa Teresa #32-41; check opening hours.
