High season in Cartagena: Christmas, New Year and Holy Week

December, January and Holy Week are when Cartagena fills up. Here's how to plan.

Leer en español →

From mid-December to mid-January, and again in Holy Week, Cartagena welcomes half of Colombia and plenty of travellers from abroad. The city fills with energy, and with people: planning ahead makes all the difference.

Christmas

The Old City lights up and the plazas fill early. 25 December 2026 falls on a Friday, so Christmas is a long weekend.

New Year

1 January 2027 is also a Friday. New Year's Eve is the most sought-after night in the city: to dine at Casa Salvaje, join the guest list and hear about our end-of-year plans first.

Holy Week 2027

Holy Thursday and Good Friday fall on 25 and 26 March 2027. It's another of Cartagena's busiest weeks.

High-season tips

  • Book everything early: flights, hotel and tables.
  • Walk the Old City: it's faster than any car.
  • Mind the holidays in the long-weekend calendar.
  • Special dates: hours can change; confirm on WhatsApp and check opening hours.
