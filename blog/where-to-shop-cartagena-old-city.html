Where to shop in Cartagena: Colombian design in the old city

From St Dom to Johanna Ortiz: the shops that dress the walled city.

Leer en español →

The old city isn't just for walking and eating: it's also the best place in Colombia to shop for design. These are the shops that live up to the walled city, all within walking distance of Casa Salvaje.

St Dom

The concept store that put Cartagena on the design map. A colonial house with a patio and garden full of fashion, jewellery, books, art and objects by Colombian and Latin American designers, steps from Plaza Santo Domingo. It opened in 2014 and is the first stop to understand the country's design.

See on the map

Johanna Ortiz

The most coveted Colombian designer abroad has her own boutique on Plaza Santo Domingo. Ruffles, prints and dresses that seem made for long Caribbean nights.

Website · See on the map

Silvia Tcherassi

She shows in Paris and Milan and has her boutique inside the walled city. Light whites, fabrics that breathe and silhouettes designed for Cartagena's heat.

Website · See on the map

Lina Cantillo

Menswear made in Colombia, some of the best in the country: shirts, linen and resort pieces with real craft. Her shop is inside the Sofitel Legend Santa Clara hotel, by the pool.

Website · See on the map

Soloio

Spanish menswear made in Italy, with linen as its signature: shirts, trousers and shorts for the heat. Its shop is by Plaza San Pedro, and Soloio Mare does swimwear.

Website · See on the map

The Relicka

Clothing 100% hand-woven by Colombian women who head their households: knits, crochet and soft, breathable fabrics. Its Caribeña collection was born for this climate.

Website · See on the map

Bahama Mama

Swimwear and beachwear made in Cartagena since 2014, with recycled fabrics and prints from the Colombian Caribbean. What to pack before heading out to the islands.

Website · See on the map

For the home

Casa Chiqui

Cartagena's most iconic home store, on Calle de la Universidad: furniture and objects gathered from Bali to Morocco, plus its own jewellery and accessories made by Colombian artisans. To take a piece of the house home.

Website · See on the map

Atticca

Colonial antiques on Calle del Cuartel, in the heart of the old city: furniture, objects and pieces from the same era as the walled city's houses. For anyone after something no one else will have.

Instagram · See on the map

A tip

Shops open by day and most close as the afternoon fades: shop early and end with an aperitivo at La Domenica or dinner at Kanibal or SLVJ. We're at Calle Santa Teresa #32-41; check opening hours and see where to eat in the old city.
