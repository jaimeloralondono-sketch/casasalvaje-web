The music of Casa Salvaje: a playlist for every world

Five worlds, five sounds. All our playlists are on Spotify, so the house plays wherever you are.

Leer en español →

At Casa Salvaje the music changes with the hour and with the door you walk through. Morning coffee doesn't sound like Kanibal's fire, and dinner at SLVJ doesn't sound like the night, when the room becomes a club. That's why every world has its own playlist.

A playlist for every world

  • Casa Salvaje. The one that brings the whole house together: listen on Spotify.
  • La Domenica. A Sunday that never ends: coffee, a long lunch and aperitivo as the afternoon fades. Two playlists: one and another.
  • Kanibal. The pulse of fire and instinct, in two playlists.
  • SLVJ. Two faces, like the night: Dinner, to behave at the table, and Party, for when you no longer have to.
  • ONO. Where ritual meets desire: the ONO playlist.

Follow us on Spotify

They all live on our Spotify profile. Follow it and take the house home, to the car or to the pre-party.

The house plays on the web too

Every page of casa-salvaje.com has its own music: tap anywhere and it starts, always on a random song. The "Music" button lets you pause, skip and, at SLVJ, choose between Dinner and Party.

And in the club, Funktion-One

Thursday to Saturday, when SLVJ becomes a club, the music plays on a Funktion-One system. Read why in the sound of SLVJ.

Getting there

We're at Calle Santa Teresa #32-41; check opening hours and read what a night at SLVJ looks like.
