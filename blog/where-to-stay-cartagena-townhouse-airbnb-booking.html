Where to stay in Cartagena: Townhouse, vacation rentals, Airbnb and Booking

A hotel with a rooftop, a colonial house with a pool for the whole group or a flat in Getsemaní: how to choose, and what to check before you book.

Leer en español →

Cartagena has every kind of place to stay: boutique hotels in colonial houses, whole houses with pools for big groups and thousands of flats on the platforms. The right choice depends on who you're travelling with and how much you want to walk at night.

Townhouse: a small hotel, a rooftop and jazz

In the walled city, steps from Plaza Fernández de Madrid, Townhouse is a boutique hotel with a handful of rooms and two bars: a rooftop with plunge pools and views over the tiled roofs, and a lobby bar that turns speakeasy with jazz at weekends. Ideal for couples or friends who want a hotel with atmosphere without leaving the Old City.

Colonial vacation rentals

For groups (families, birthdays, bachelor and bachelorette parties), a whole colonial house is often the best option: several en-suite bedrooms, a courtyard, a pool or terrace and, often, housekeeping, breakfast and a concierge for transfers, island boats and restaurant bookings. Local agencies specialise in houses in the Old City and Getsemaní, and many also list on the platforms.

Airbnb, Booking and Vrbo

  • Airbnb: the widest choice of houses and flats in the Old City, Getsemaní, Bocagrande and Manga, plus experiences with local guides.
  • Booking: hotels and apartments side by side; handy for comparing prices and booking with flexible cancellation.
  • Vrbo: focused on whole homes, good for large groups.

What to check before you book

  • National Tourism Registry (RNT): in Colombia, tourist accommodation, including holiday homes, must be registered. Many listings show their number; ask for it if it's missing.
  • Air conditioning in every bedroom, not just the main one.
  • Exact location: the Old City and San Diego are walkable; Bocagrande and Manga are a short drive.
  • Noise: the Old City has nightlife at weekends. If you sleep early, look for quieter streets such as those in San Diego.
  • Stairs and pools: many colonial houses have two or three floors and no lift.
  • Recent reviews and cancellation policies, especially in high season and on long weekends.
