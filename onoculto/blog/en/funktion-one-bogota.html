The body listens too: ONO's Funktion-One sound in Bogotá

Bass you feel without being deafened, and a dance floor that won't sit still. Why ONO's nights sound with Funktion-One.

By ONO Hosomaki Bar

After eleven, you don't hear the bass: you feel it. It climbs through the floor, settles in your chest and, suddenly, nobody in the room is standing still. A place can have the best food and the best crowd, but if the sound fails, the night falls apart. That's why, when ONO turns from dinner to the ritual, it sounds with Funktion-One.

What is Funktion-One

Funktion-One is a British sound-system brand that became the benchmark for electronic music and for the most demanding clubs in the world. Its promise is clarity: bass you feel without being deafened, and voices and instruments you can tell apart even at high volume.

It was founded in 1992 by Tony Andrews and John Newsham, the same engineers who had earlier created Turbosound, and the "k" in the name is a nod to seventies funk. Its obsession is the same as any good dance floor's: bass you feel in your body, and nobody leaving with ruined ears.

On a good night, you don't notice the sound: you dance it.

How ONO sounds

ONO sheds its skin through the night. From 7 to 11 pm, over dinner, the music keeps you company: enveloping rhythms at conversation level. From 11, the lights drop and the sound rises. Tuesday and Wednesday it's electronic; Thursday to Saturday, reggaeton. The table becomes a dance floor without anyone leaving their seat.

Listen before you come

ONO's music plays outside the room too: the ONO playlist is on Spotify. And to live it in person, read what the party is like at ONO, Tuesday to Saturday.
