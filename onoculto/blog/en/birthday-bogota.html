Celebrating a birthday in Bogotá: dinner and party in one place

The best birthday plan is the one where nobody has to move. At ONO, the dinner table becomes the party table.

By ONO Hosomaki Bar

Planning a birthday in Bogotá usually has one problem: dinner in one place, the party in another, and half the group lost on the way. At ONO there's no need to move. The night starts at the table and, at 11 pm, that same table becomes part of the dance floor.

How the night comes together

At 7 pm, dinner to share: hosomaki, nigiri, sashimi and a house roll for the middle of the table. Then signature cocktails or sake for the toast. At 11 pm the lights drop, the music rises and the ritual begins. Tuesday and Wednesday it's electronic; Thursday to Saturday, reggaeton.

The dinner table is the party table.

Which night to choose

To talk and dance without crowds, Tuesday or Wednesday. For a packed floor and weekend energy, Thursday to Saturday. Read what the party is like at ONO, Tuesday to Saturday, to choose.

How to book for a group

For groups and celebrations, book ahead. You can book dinner online, and for party tables, bottles or special touches, message us on Instagram at @ono.culto. Remember ONO is for guests 25 and over and ID is checked at the door.

ONO is at Calle 84A #9-11, Bogotá, Tuesday to Saturday, 7 pm to 3 am.
