Reggaeton in Bogotá: Thursday, Friday and Saturday at ONO

Thursday to Saturday, ONO sounds like reggaeton. Dinner at 7, the floor at 11, and a night in no hurry to end.

By ONO Hosomaki Bar

In Bogotá, Thursday is already the weekend, and at ONO you can tell. Thursday to Saturday, when dinner ends and the lights drop, the room plays reggaeton and the floor fills early. It's the night to come as a group, order a bottle and stop checking the time.

What the night is like

At 7 pm, dinner: hosomaki, nigiri and sashimi to share, and signature cocktails. At 11 pm the ritual begins: the music rises, the tables become a dance floor and the red glow of the shoji does the rest. Everything plays on a Funktion-One system built for dancing until 3 am without ruining your ears.

Thursday, Friday and Saturday: the floor fills early.

Tables, groups and celebrations

For these nights, book ahead. You can book dinner online, and for party tables, bottles, groups or celebrations, message us on Instagram at @ono.culto. If you're coming for a birthday, read how to celebrate a birthday in Bogotá at ONO.

Before you go

ONO is for guests 25 and over, and ID is checked at the door. We're at Calle 84A #9-11, Bogotá. And if you'd rather have electronic, come on Tuesday or Wednesday: read electronic music on weeknights in Bogotá.

Your table is waiting.
