Electronic music on weeknights in Bogotá: Tuesday and Wednesday at ONO

For people who don't wait for Friday. On Tuesday and Wednesday, ONO sounds electronic: house, deep house and beats on a Funktion-One system.

By ONO Hosomaki Bar

Some people wait for Friday to go out, and some know the best nights happen midweek: shorter lines, more room on the floor and a crowd that comes for the music. For them, ONO's Tuesdays and Wednesdays are electronic nights.

What plays

House, deep house and beats to dance to without rushing. The night starts gently, over dinner, and from 11 pm the sound rises and the bar becomes a club. Everything plays on a Funktion-One system, the kind of sound found in the world's most demanding clubs: bass you feel in your chest and clear vocals even at high volume. Read the body listens too: ONO's Funktion-One sound to learn more.

On weeknights, the floor belongs to people who come for the music.

Dinner before dancing

The good thing about ONO is that you don't have to move: the night starts at 7 pm with hosomaki, nigiri and signature cocktails, and carries on at the same table when the music begins. If you don't know what to order, read our guide to sushi in Bogotá.

And the rest of the week

Thursday to Saturday, ONO changes its sound to reggaeton. That's how the week works: Tuesday and Wednesday, electronic; Thursday, Friday and Saturday, reggaeton. Read what the party is like at ONO, Tuesday to Saturday.

ONO is open Tuesday to Saturday, 7 pm to 3 am, for guests 25 and over, at Calle 84A #9-11, Bogotá. The ONO playlist is on Spotify so you arrive in the mood.

Your table is waiting.
