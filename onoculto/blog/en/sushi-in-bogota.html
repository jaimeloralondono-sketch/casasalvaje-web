Sushi in Bogotá: hosomaki, nigiri and signature cocktails at ONO

Hosomaki, nigiri and sashimi made with precision, a cocktail bar beside them and a night that doesn't end at eleven.

By ONO Hosomaki Bar

There's sushi you order in on any given Tuesday, and there's sushi that's the excuse for a whole night out. At ONO Hosomaki Bar, in the north of Bogotá, it's the second kind: you arrive for dinner under low light, between shoji screens and dark timber, and at eleven the music rises and the table turns into a party.

A menu that starts with the simple things

ONO's kitchen starts from Japanese simplicity: hosomaki, nigiri, sashimi and hand rolls made with precision. Every piece has one clear star. The rice is seasoned with vinegar and handled warm; the fish is cut to order; what goes on top, a house eel sauce, a panko crunch, a touch of truffle, changes everything without hiding anything.

Among the house rolls are the Kaori roll, with salmon and tuna in soy paper, avocado, panko crunch, sesame mayo and truffle butter; the Sakana umami, with tuna, hamachi and salmon, cream cheese and spring onion, finished with eel sauce; and the Black truffled, one bite of rice, salmon and truffle butter flamed at the table.

Hosomaki, nigiri, sashimi: what to order

If it's your first time, start simple and let the fish speak.

Hosomaki is the thin roll, nori outside and a single ingredient in the middle: the best way to judge the quality of the fish. Nigiri is a pillow of rice with a slice of fish on top, and sashimi is the fish alone, no rice. To share, order a little of everything and repeat what you like most. To learn more about the roll that gives the house its name, read what is hosomaki.

Signature cocktails and sake

The bar matters as much as the kitchen. There are signature cocktails, classics with an ONO twist, and sake to go with dinner. Early on the bar is quick and elegant; at night, when the bar becomes a club, it's the heart of the room.

When to go and how to book

ONO is open Tuesday to Saturday, 7 pm to 3 am, for guests 25 and over. Dinner runs from 7 to 11 pm; then the ritual begins, with the music up and the dance floor open. We're at Calle 84A #9-11, Bogotá. Book your table online and come hungry.
