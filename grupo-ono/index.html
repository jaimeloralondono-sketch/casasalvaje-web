About us

We started with a hosomaki bar in Bogotá. Then came a whole house in Cartagena. Always the same idea: that the kitchen, the bar, the music and the service tell one story.

Grupo ONO makes places with character. Each brand has its own kitchen, look and rhythm, and behind all of them is one team obsessed with produce and with a night done right.
