About

We started in 2025 with a hosomaki bar in Bogotá. In 2026 came a whole house in Cartagena.

Always the same idea: that the kitchen, the bar, the music and the service tell one story. Grupo ONO makes places with character. Each brand has its own kitchen, look and rhythm, and behind all of them is one team obsessed with produce and with a night done right.

About Grupo ONO · 72 words

Grupo ONO is a hospitality group founded by Jaime Lora and Dilan Carrillo. It began in Bogotá in November 2025 with ONO Hosomaki Bar, a Japanese-inspired hosomaki bar and night club. On 25 September 2026 it opened Casa Salvaje in Cartagena's Old City, a colonial house with four worlds under one roof: Kanibal and SLVJ, both open; La Domenica, opening on a date to be confirmed; and ONO Cartagena, opening November–December 2026.
